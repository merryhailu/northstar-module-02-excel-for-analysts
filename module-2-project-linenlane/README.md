# LinenLane Analysis

## 1. What is this?

This engagement analyzed LinenLane’s marketing and customer data. The deliverables include an Excel analysis workbook, a business memo, and supporting documentation of the analysis and AI use.

## 2. What's in the folder?

- `linenlane-q4-2024_final.xlsx` — Excel workbook containing the data analysis, formulas, pivots, dashboard, and statistical analysis.
- `beatrix_holloway_memo.md` — Business memo summarizing the key findings and recommendations for Beatrix Holloway.
- `module-2-ai-use-disclosure.md` — Documentation of how AI was used, with 6 elements including task, tool, prompts, original outputs, verification steps, and Decisions.
- `README.md` — Overview of the engagement, folder contents, headline findings, and AI use.

## 3. The headline findings

Variant B generated about 70 more purchases per 28,000 emails, a roughly 13.5% lift in purchase rate, with a p-value of 0.034 and a 95% confidence interval of +0.02 to +0.48 percentage points. For click-through rate, Variant B generated about 48 more clicks per 28,000 emails, but the roughly 2% lift was not statistically significant (p = 0.47), so the result should be tested again before making a permanent change.

AI was used on five distinct tasks during this engagement, including formula generation, analysis support, and statistical interpretation. Full details with prompts, outputs, and verification steps are documented in `module-2-ai-use-disclosure.md`.
