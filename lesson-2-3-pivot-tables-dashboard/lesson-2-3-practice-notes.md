# One pattern or finding from the marketing pivot

The pivot shows that marketing spend generally increased from January to December across all channels, although some channels had small fluctuations during the year. Search and Paid Social had the highest spending compared with Affiliates and Display, with Search showing a particularly noticeable spike in October. Spending also increased across several channels during the fourth quarter (October–December), suggesting a stronger marketing investment toward the end of the year.

# One pattern or finding from the returns pivot

The returns pivot shows that damaged items have the highest total refund amount and the highest number of returns. Customer changed mind is the second-highest return reason in both total refund amount and return count. This suggests that damaged items should be a key focus for reducing return costs, while customer-change-of-mind returns may require a different approach, such as clearer product information or descriptions.
